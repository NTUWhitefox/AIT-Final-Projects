# Quaternius pedestrians

Selected `Casual_Male.fbx`, `Casual_Female.fbx` and `Casual2_Male.fbx` from the [Ultimate Animated Character Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter), by Quaternius. The creator identifies the pack as **CC0 1.0**. The original license text and source hashes are stored alongside these files.

Retrieved from the creator-linked public FBX folder on 2026-11-08. No other characters or formats from the pack are imported.

Project derivatives convert materials to URP, apply a simplified skin palette, normalize pedestrian height, and extract looping idle/walk clips for controlled sidewalk/crossing movement. These are stylized, low-poly background people, not scanned likenesses or validated human-behavior models.
