Conversion: Tools/convert_scooter.py at repository root (see that script).
Converted to native OBJ; coordinates, winding and texture-transform UVs adapted.
PNG texture bytes are extracted unmodified. Materials are adapted to URP Lit.
Not all glTF optical material extensions are reproduced.

The test scene hides the deployed stand and static instrument needle/ticks and adds
digital speed text, steering/wheel pivots and monoscopic rear-view camera screens.
No motorcycle geometry was authored for this project. This generic retro scooter
is not a verified replica of a Taiwanese 125/150 cc production model.
