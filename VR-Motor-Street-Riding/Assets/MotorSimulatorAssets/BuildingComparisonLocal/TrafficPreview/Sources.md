# Kobo traffic preview sources

- Quaternius, CC0: [NormalCar1](https://poly.pizza/m/Cz6yDaUcM9) and [SUV](https://poly.pizza/m/xsMtZhBkxL). Roof height, lamp surfaces, paint and wheel pivots adapted.
- Quaternius, CC0: [Ultimate Animated Character Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter). Casual2_Male posed and baked, helmet and visor added.
- Quaternius, CC0: [Universal Base Characters Standard](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters) and [Universal Animation Library Standard](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library). Adult pedestrians use the free rigged bases with project clothing surfaces and resized silhouettes. UAL idle and walk were retargeted, grounded and baked with animation speed matched to path movement.
- TDAL, user-supplied Scooter D FBX: [license agreement](https://www.tdal.tw/tw/webcontent/license_agreement). Original geometry split by steering and axle role, with new flat materials and grips. The source license also applies to the derivatives.
- The scene references the installed Kobo buildings under their bundled license.
